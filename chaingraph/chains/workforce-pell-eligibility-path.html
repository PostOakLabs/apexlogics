Workforce Pell Eligibility Path

Gated two-step chain: screen Workforce Pell Grant eligibility for a short-term credential program (150–599 clock hours, 8–14 weeks). An ineligible result routes to end — the chain stops rather than composing a downstream ROI comparison against a grant that will not fund the program. Only an eligible or review result continues to the Credential ROI Suite.
