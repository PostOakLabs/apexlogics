Learn OpenChainGraph, the standard behind this suite

OpenChainGraph (OCG) is an open standard for turning a browser tool's output into a verifiable, agent-callable artifact: every step hashes its inputs and result, and that hash chains into the next step, so a reviewer or an AI agent can confirm what ran without re-running it or trusting a screenshot. ApexLogics is one live implementation; the standard's home base, tool catalog, and full technical writeup live at AINumbers.co.
