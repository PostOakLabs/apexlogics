Use this when…

Common scenarios across the five disciplines.

📐
Planning a lesson from scratch
Scaffold using Understanding by Design (UbD), PPP, TBL, or ESA. Generate objectives, assessment evidence, and a learning plan aligned to a framework.
🗣️
Teaching ESL students with L1 interference
Identify Mandarin or Spanish transfer error patterns, decide when and how to correct using the Lyster-Ranta framework, and level texts to the right CEFR band.
📜
Analyzing a primary source document
SOAPSTone analysis, provenance check, bias inventory, and corroboration prompts. Plus historiographical interpretation from five competing schools.
🏟️
Teaching sport management economics
Compare franchise vs. promotion/relegation models, design a survey study, model payroll efficiency with embedded MLB data, or debate automated officiating.
📈
Teaching or reviewing economics concepts
Interactive supply-demand diagrams, Phillips curve scatter plots, recession anatomy bar charts, game theory payoff matrices, and a 30-card key-terms flashcard deck.
