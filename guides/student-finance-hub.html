Common decision scenarios covered by this category.

📋
Estimating financial aid before applying
Simulate your Student Aid Index under the current FAFSA formula. See Pell eligibility and federal aid before schools send award letters.
🏦
Planning 529 contributions
Project 529 balance at enrollment across contribution scenarios. Model state tax deductions and coverage percentage by school tier.
🏆
Prioritizing scholarship applications
Rank scholarships by net ROI — award value vs. time invested vs. estimated win rate. Build a pipeline that pays.
💳
Choosing a loan repayment plan
Compare IDR, PSLF, standard, and graduated repayment by monthly payment, total interest paid, and forgiveness value.
