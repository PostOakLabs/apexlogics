Use this when…

Common decision scenarios covered by this category.

💰
Filing crypto taxes
Size a harvestable loss for the year and see where staking income lands before filing.
🧭
Drawing down retirement accounts
Compute the required distribution, then check conversion room against the next IRMAA cliff.
🏠
Buying or holding a home
Run the lender ratios on a purchase and see the full multi-year cost of staying in a home.
🤝
Dividing assets in a divorce
Build the marital balance sheet and weigh keeping the house against selling it.
🏦
Selling a business
Normalize owner pay and value the company before modeling the tax on a sale.
📜
Planning an estate
Size the probate pool and model estate tax after deductions and exemptions.
🌿
Funding long-term care
Compare care settings and see how long private pay lasts before Medicaid eligibility.
