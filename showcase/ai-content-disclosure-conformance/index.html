AI Content Disclosure
(AI Act Article 50)

Pick the content type you are shipping and check off which marking elements you already have in place: machine readable marking, a disclosure visible to the user, disclosure at first interaction, provenance metadata, and whether human review is documented. This tool routes the content type to a full or reduced disclosure regime, works out how many of that regime's required elements you have satisfied, and names exactly which ones are still missing. The routing table here is this demo's own frozen reading of the marking duties. It is illustrative and not legal advice.
