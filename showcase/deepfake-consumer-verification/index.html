Deepfake Consumer
Verification

Answer five yes, no, or unknown questions about a clip before you reshare it: does it carry a credential, does that credential match the source, can you identify the publisher, is a disclosure label visible, and did you find an independent report. Each answer scores 2 for yes, 1 for unknown, 0 for no, and this tool sums them into a confidence band built only from what you were actually able to check. Absence of a credential is not proof of manipulation, and presence of one is not proof of truth. This scores what you could check, nothing more.
