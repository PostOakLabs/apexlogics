Material Passport Lineage

A digital product passport has to say where a material spec came from, not just what it is now. Fill in a short revision walk, up to three steps, and this page derives a descriptor for each step that stamps the one before it. The walk is reproducible from the entered values, so a revision claim can be checked instead of taken at face value.
