Export Completeness Receipt

You asked a company for your data and something arrived. Enter the section labels the export contains, the record count it claims, and the digest the provider published with it, and this tool checks the sections against what you requested, recomputes the receipt, and tells you whether what you hold matches what was published. Re-running the same export description reproduces the same receipt and the same hash, so a later dispute has something specific to point at.

Data: section set comparison and receipt digest · no external data
