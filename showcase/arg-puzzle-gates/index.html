ARG Puzzle Gates

An alternate-reality game runs on gated chains. Solve the clue, submit the answer, and its fingerprint is checked against the stage key. A correct answer's OpenChainGraph gate routes the chain forward to the next stage; a wrong answer routes to end. This is the clearest both-branch decision-gate demo in the creative set: the route is a pure function of your answer, recomputable by any verifier.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
