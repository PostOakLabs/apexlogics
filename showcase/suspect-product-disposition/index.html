Suspect-Product
Disposition Receipt

Quarantine decisions get made at a receiving dock and reviewed months later. Paste the product identifier from the package, add the receipt-time observations, and this page parses the identifier, checks the check digit and the expiry, and stamps a disposition receipt with a reproducible hash. Anyone re-running the same identifier and the same reference day gets the same verdict.

Data: 21 U.S.C. §360eee (DSCSA) · GS1 General Specifications
