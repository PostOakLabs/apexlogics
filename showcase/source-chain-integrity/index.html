Source Chain Integrity

A desk needs to show a document timeline held together without publishing anything about the source. Enter the fingerprints of the documents you received, in the order you received them, with an integer day index for each, and this page folds them into a timeline key and checks the ordering. Nothing identifying is entered, and the timeline is reproducible by anyone holding the same fingerprints.

Data: no external data
