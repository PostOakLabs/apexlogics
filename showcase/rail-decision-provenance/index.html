OCG-Industries Showcase · Cluster ⑥ Payments and Financial Infrastructure

Rail Decision
Provenance

The choice of a payment rail is usually a judgement call that leaves no trace. Enter the rail you are proposing and the properties you are claiming for it, and this tool scores the claim against frozen weights, routes a readiness tier, and stamps the whole decision into a hash. Re-running the same claim reproduces the same tier and the same hash, so the reasoning can be re-derived instead of re-argued.
