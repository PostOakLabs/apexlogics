Skills-Verification
Coverage Gate

Skills-based hiring falls apart when "verified" means whatever the reviewer felt. Enter the skills a role requires and how many of them are covered by credentials that actually verify, and this page returns a coverage figure in basis points against a threshold you set in advance, stamped with a reproducible hash. It scores verified credential coverage for a role. It does not score a person.

Data: no external data

This page scores verified credential coverage, not a person. There are no fields here for age, sex, race, national origin, disability, religion, or any proxy for them, and none may be added. The result is reported only as "meets the declared coverage threshold" or "does not meet it," never as qualified, unqualified, recommended, or rejected. Any employment decision is made by a human who is accountable for it, not by this page.
