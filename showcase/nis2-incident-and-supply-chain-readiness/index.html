OCG-Industries Showcase · Cluster 14 NIS2

NIS2 Incident Reporting
and Supply-Chain Readiness

Enter the hour you became aware of an incident and the hour each report was actually filed, and this tool stamps the Article 23 early warning, incident notification, and final report milestones against the 24 hour, 72 hour, and 30 day windows. Check off four supply-chain readiness items alongside it to see both halves of your readiness picture in one place.

Data: Regulation (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2) Art.21, Art.23

Article 23 Incident Clock STEP 1
Hour zero of the incident clock
-1 means not filed. Due at awareness plus 24 hours.
-1 means not filed. Due at awareness plus 72 hours.
-1 means not filed. Due at awareness plus 720 hours (30 days).
