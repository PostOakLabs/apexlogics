Civic Decision
Record

A published decision earns trust when a reader can see what it was based on and what right of challenge follows. Enter the decision reference, the statutory basis, the factors weighed, and the day the decision was published, and this tool checks the record against a frozen completeness rule, computes the appeal window close from the published day index, and stamps the record. Re-running the same record reproduces the same result and the same hash.
