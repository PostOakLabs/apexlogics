IFRS 17 Measurement
Model Classification Receipt

Enter the contract facts for a group of insurance contracts and this tool walks the IFRS 17 classification logic to determine whether the group falls under the General Measurement Model, the Premium Allocation Approach, or the Variable Fee Approach. It also returns the onerous contract test requirement, the loss component flag, and the exact decision path taken, so the classification can be re-run and checked at audit.

Data: IFRS 17 Insurance Contracts paras 29 to 59, B101 to B118
