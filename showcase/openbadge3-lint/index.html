Open Badges 3.0
Issuance Lint

A badge that does not parse as a verifiable credential is a picture with ambition. Paste a credential and this page checks its context URIs, required properties, achievement shape, and validity-window ordering, then stamps the findings with a reproducible hash. The credential itself never leaves the page and never enters the hash. Only the rule outcomes are hashed.
