Receipt

Most failed replications fail on something the paper never wrote down. Enter the declared inputs of a run, the seed, and the receipt digest the authors published, and this tool checks the declaration against a frozen minimum, recomputes the receipt, and tells you whether what you were handed matches what was published. Re-running the same declaration reproduces the same receipt and the same hash.
