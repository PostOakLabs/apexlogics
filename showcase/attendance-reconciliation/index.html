Attendance Reconciliation

Two parties report two attendance figures and there is no neutral arbiter. Enter the tickets issued, the scans recorded, the comps and the refunds, and this page reconciles them into a single reported figure, flags any line that cannot be accounted for, and folds the whole reconciliation into a key. The arithmetic is integer exact, so both parties get the same answer from the same numbers.

Scope. Every figure here is entered by hand and the day index is a user-chosen integer. This shows that two accounts can be reconciled to the same answer, not that either account is truthful, and it does not establish when anything happened.

Data: no external data

Inputs STEP 1
An integer day index of your own choosing. This page uses no clock.
