Reserve Attestation
Integrity

An attestation you cannot re-check is a PDF you have to believe. Enter the attestation identifier, the reserve line item labels it discloses, and the digest that was published alongside it, and this tool recomputes the digest from the labels you entered and tells you whether the disclosure you are holding matches the one that was published. Re-running the same labels reproduces the same digest and the same hash.

Data vintage: 2026
