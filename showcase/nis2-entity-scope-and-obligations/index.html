OCG-Industries Showcase · Cluster 14 NIS2

NIS2 Entity Scope
and Obligations

Enter your sector, headcount, annual turnover, balance sheet total, and whether you are the sole provider of your service in your member state, and this tool classifies your entity against the directive's own Annex I and Annex II sector lists and its size-cap rule. It returns your size band, whether you fall in scope, and whether the essential or important obligation set applies. National transposition varies, so this is an illustrative classification against the directive's own size-cap rule, not a jurisdiction-specific determination.

Data: Regulation (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS2) Art.3, Annex I, Annex II · Recommendation 2003/361/EC size criteria
