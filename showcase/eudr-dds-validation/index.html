EUDR Due Diligence
Statement Validation

Check off the eight elements a due diligence statement needs, each carrying its own weight toward a 100 point total, and this tool tells you the score, names exactly which elements are still missing, and flags whether any of the three elements that block filing outright are unchecked. A statement either has everything it needs or it names its own gap, and either way the same checklist re-run gives the same score. The weights shown here are this demo's own frozen illustration of relative importance, not a figure published by the Commission.

Data: Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 (EUDR) Art.4, Art.9, Annex II

Statement Elements STEP 1

Geolocation plots, country of production, and risk assessment each block filing outright if left unchecked.
