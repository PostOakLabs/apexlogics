OCG-Industries Showcase · Cluster ⑳ Healthcare Administration

Prior-Auth
Metrics Pack

Public reporting of prior-authorization outcomes turns an internal spreadsheet into a published figure. Paste a batch of decision outcomes and this page returns approval, denial, and overturn rates as integer basis points plus a mean decision time scaled by 100, then stamps the pack with a reproducible hash. Nothing is rounded away before hashing, so a reviewer can recompute every rate from the same batch. This page aggregates numbers only; it gives no medical advice.

Data: CMS-0057-F · figures user-entered

Decision Batch STEP 1
Format: decision,decision_days where decision is approved, denied, or overturned. Example line: approved,4. This fixture batch works out to a 60% approval rate, a 20% denial rate, a 20% overturn rate, and a 5.00-day mean decision time.
