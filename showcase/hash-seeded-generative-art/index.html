Hash-Seeded Generative Art

One seed, one artwork, one hash. The seed and a complexity setting deterministically draw the SVG below through a 32-bit generator; the same seed always regenerates the identical image and recomputes the identical execution hash. Change the seed, change the art and the hash. This is the purest demonstration that an OpenChainGraph artifact is reproducible from its inputs alone.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
