OCG-Industries Showcase · Cluster 15 EMIR

EMIR Reconciliation
and Lifecycle

Enter your side and your counterparty's side of six reconciliation fields, plus a valuation tolerance, and this tool compares them field by field: text fields trimmed and uppercased, integers exactly, and valuation within your stated tolerance. It names every mismatched field, signs the notional and valuation deltas, and returns a reconciliation state so a break is attributable rather than disputed.
