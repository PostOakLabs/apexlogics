MBA Scholarship
Strategy Engine

Scholarship dollars are won at two decision points: before you apply (Round 1 applicants access the full merit budget and enjoy a 4–7 point admission rate edge at most M7 schools) and after you're admitted (roughly half of merit-scholarship counters at T11–25 schools succeed). This engine models both — the round-timing expected value and the offer-negotiation expected value — in dollars.
