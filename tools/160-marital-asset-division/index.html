Marital Asset
Division

Lays out a marital balance sheet and models how it might split under either a community property regime or an equitable distribution regime. Separate property is tracked apart from marital property, illiquid and tax-deferred assets are shown at an after-tax equivalent, and the result is an illustrative allocation, not a settlement.
