Small Business · Exit Planning

Owner Salary
Normalization

Owner pay in a small business is rarely a market wage. It is a mix of salary, distributions, and personal expenses run through the company. This tool separates what a replacement manager would actually cost from what the owner takes, so both seller discretionary earnings and normalized EBITDA come out on a comparable basis.

Data: 2026 (SS wage base $184,500; market wage is user-supplied)
