⚠ RAP Replaces SAVE — New Borrower Default (July 1, 2026) Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA 2025), the SAVE plan ended and was replaced by the Repayment Assistance Plan (RAP) effective July 1, 2026. New Direct Loan borrowers default to RAP — not the Standard Plan. RAP parameters: 1–10% of AGI (income-based scale), $10/month floor, 30-year forgiveness, qualifies for PSLF. This tool models Standard, Graduated, Extended, IBR, PAYE, ICR, and RAP (PSLF tab). For full RAP vs. Standard comparison use the RAP vs. Standard Decision Engine (AL-96, coming soon).

About PSLF

Public Service Loan Forgiveness cancels the remaining balance on your Direct Loans after 120 qualifying monthly payments under a qualifying repayment plan while working full-time for a qualifying employer.

Qualifying employers: Federal, state, local, tribal government; qualifying 501(c)(3) non-profits; AmeriCorps; Peace Corps.

Qualifying plans: IBR, PAYE, ICR, RAP, and Standard 10-year. Graduated and Extended plans do NOT qualify. (RAP added as PSLF-qualifying per OBBBA 2025.)
