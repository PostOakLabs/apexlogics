Medicare Out-of-Pocket
Exposure Modeler

Medicare pays most of the bill, but the part it does not pay has no annual ceiling on the medical side unless you carry a supplement or an Advantage plan. This modeler walks a year of utilization through the actual 2026 cost-sharing rules, benefit period by benefit period, and shows what a routine year, a rough year, and a worst-case year each cost you.
