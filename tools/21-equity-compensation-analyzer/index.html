⚠ Legal & Tax Notice — Read Before Use

This tool provides illustrative estimates only and does not constitute tax, legal, or financial advice. Equity compensation tax treatment is complex and highly fact-specific. Always consult a qualified tax advisor, CPA, or attorney before making exercise, hold, or sale decisions. AMT calculations are simplified estimates; actual AMT liability depends on full tax return context. ISO qualifying disposition rules require both a 2-year hold from grant date and 1-year hold from exercise date.
