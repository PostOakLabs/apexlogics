    State Conformity Note

    Many states do not conform to IRC §1202, meaning your state will tax the full gain even if the federal exclusion applies. Prominent non-conforming states include California, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and Alabama. Always verify with a tax advisor for your specific state.

    💡 If your state doesn't conform and you have an 83(b) election on record, your state LTCG will be calculated on the full gain. Factor this into your exit planning — the federal saving may be partially offset by state tax.
