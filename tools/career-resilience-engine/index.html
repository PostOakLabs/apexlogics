📊 What the Score Measures
The Frey & Osborne (2013) model estimates the probability that an occupation's tasks could be automated using machine learning, robotics, or advanced algorithms. Three "bottleneck" factors reduce automation risk:
👁 Perception & manipulation 💡 Creative intelligence 🤝 Social intelligence
Occupations requiring any of these are harder to automate. Routine cognitive and physical tasks — regardless of skill level — score highest.
⚠️ Important Context
These scores reflect the 2013 technology frontier — before large language models and generative AI. Some low-risk occupations (editors, writers) may face new exposure; some high-risk ones (truck drivers) have been slower to automate than predicted. Use this as a structural baseline, not a prediction. The national average across all 702 occupations is approximately 0.47 (47%).
