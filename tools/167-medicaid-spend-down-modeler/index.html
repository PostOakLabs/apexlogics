Long-Term Care · Medicaid Planning

Medicaid
Spend-Down Modeler

Long-term-care Medicaid requires countable assets below a low limit, and a married applicant's community spouse is allowed to keep a resource allowance in between a federal floor and ceiling. This models the gap between current countable assets and the limit, and shows how long private pay would last before eligibility. It covers ten states, listed in the selector below, and rules vary by state and change often.

Data: 2026 (CSRA $32,532 minimum / $162,660 maximum; individual asset limit $2,000; ten-state scope)

This tool models ten states: California, Florida, Illinois, Michigan, New Jersey, New York, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Texas, and Washington. Selecting any other state applies federal default figures that may not match that state's rules.
