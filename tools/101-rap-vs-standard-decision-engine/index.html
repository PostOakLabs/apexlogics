RAP uses a 1–10% of AGI sliding scale, $10/month floor, and 30-year forgiveness. This engine models both plans across your full repayment horizon so you can make the right choice.

About PSLF + RAP

Public Service Loan Forgiveness cancels the remaining balance on your Direct Loans after 120 qualifying monthly payments while working full-time for a qualifying employer. RAP qualifies for PSLF under OBBBA 2025. PSLF forgiveness is tax-free.

Qualifying employers: Federal, state, local, tribal government; qualifying 501(c)(3) non-profits; AmeriCorps; Peace Corps.

Qualifying plans: RAP, IBR, PAYE, ICR, and Standard 10-year. Graduated and Extended plans do NOT qualify. RAP added as qualifying plan per OBBBA 2025.
