📋 What the Fees Include
Exam fee — the voucher or application fee paid to the testing body or NCBE/state bar, as of Q4 2024. For multi-part exams (A+, CPA), the listed fee is per part; total multiplies by parts.

Study materials — your estimated spend on books, official guides, and practice tests.

Prep course — optional paid bootcamp, video course, or coaching. Many learners self-study for free; add a cost only if you plan to purchase one.

Re-attempt buffer — if you plan for more than one attempt, each additional attempt adds another exam fee.
