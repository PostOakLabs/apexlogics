#36 · AL-41 · Workforce Development

Workforce Pell
Eligibility Screener

Estimate your Workforce Pell Grant award for short-term credential programs (8–14 weeks, 150–599 clock hours), now eligible under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (P.L. 119-21), effective July 1, 2026. Bachelor's degree holders qualify for the first time.

Data: Federal Pell Grant 2026–27 · ED Dear Colleague Letter (Jan 2026) · OBBBA P.L. 119-21 (signed July 4, 2025) · Workforce Pell Final Rule (March 9, 2026)
