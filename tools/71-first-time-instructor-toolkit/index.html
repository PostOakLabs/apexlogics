First-Time Instructor Toolkit

You have full responsibility for a course — not as a TA, not as a guest lecturer, but as the instructor of record. This toolkit generates your syllabus framework, first-day plan, and grading rubric starter from your course inputs. Anchored by the experience of teaching KINE 199 at Texas A&M for three semesters as instructor of record — not TA.

📊 Syllabus design: Grunert O'Brien et al. (2008), The Course Syllabus; Fink (2003), Creating Significant Learning Experiences
🔒All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
