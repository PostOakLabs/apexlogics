Income & Tax Profile

2025 rules: DC-FSA max $5,000 (MFJ/$5,000; single $5,000; MFS $2,500). CDCTC: 20–35% credit on up to $3,000 (1 child) / $6,000 (2+ children) after subtracting FSA. Credit rate phases out: 35% at AGI ≤$15K; 20% at AGI ≥$43K+. FICA = 7.65% saved on FSA contribution (employer's FICA match also saved but not counted here). Sources: IRC §129 (DC-FSA); IRC §21 (CDCTC); IRS Pub. 503.
