Medicare Plan
Comparison

The choice between Original Medicare with a supplement and a Medicare Advantage plan is mostly a trade between a predictable premium and a variable out-of-pocket exposure. This tool prices both paths across a full year at a utilization level you set, applies the Part D out-of-pocket cap and the Advantage maximum out-of-pocket limit, and shows the cost at a typical year and at a bad year so you can see the shape of the risk, not just the average.
