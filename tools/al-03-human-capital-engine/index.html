💡 How the Model Works
Cost = exam fee + materials + optional prep course (all evening/weekend study — no opportunity cost).

Salary premium = your expected annual salary increase from holding the cert, as a % of current salary. Pre-filled from industry salary surveys; adjust to your market.

NPV = present value of all premium payments over your horizon, minus total cost. Positive = the cert pays off financially. Payback = months until cumulative premium covers cost.

Note: Salary premiums vary widely by employer, geography, and experience level. Treat embedded estimates as order-of-magnitude benchmarks.

📐 Skill Half-Life Model
Half-life is the time it takes for a skill's professional value to halve without active investment. A coding skill with a 2.5-year half-life retains 50% of its value after 2.5 years of dormancy — 25% after 5 years.

Formula: retention = e−ln(2) / halfLife × years

Career Capital Score (0–100) = weighted sum of retained value across all skills in your industry profile.

Sources: WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025 · McKinsey Global Institute Skill Shift 2021 · IBM Institute for Business Value 2024 · Deloitte Insights Human Capital Trends 2024.
