The online MBA is the fastest-growing segment — $10K–130K total cost, zero income gap, and ~90% of employers now say they'd hire online MBA graduates (GMAC 2025). But the three formats differ sharply on career-pivot power, network depth, and employer perception. This comparator models all four paths — Online, EMBA, Full-Time, and No MBA — against your goal.
