ISA is income insurance, not a loan. Unlike a loan, there is no interest rate — your obligation is a fixed percentage of income for a fixed period, capped at the repayment maximum. If your income falls, payments fall. If your income stays permanently low, you may repay far less than the funded amount. If your income rises fast, the cap protects you from unlimited liability.

Key negotiating points: income floor (minimum salary before payments start), payment cap (total max owed), and term. Always get these in writing. See the studentaid.gov ISA guidance for federal context.
