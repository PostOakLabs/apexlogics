PSLF Qualifying Payment Counter

Project your path to Public Service Loan Forgiveness: qualifying payments made vs. remaining, monthly payment under RAP/IBR/PAYE, and estimated forgiveness amount.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
2026 parameters: HHS FPL (family of 1) $15,960 · RAP (OBBBA): 1% of AGI up to 150% FPL → sliding to 10% above 450% FPL · IBR: 10% of discretionary income above 150% FPL (new borrowers) · PAYE: 10% of discretionary income above 150% FPL · PSLF: 120 qualifying payments required
