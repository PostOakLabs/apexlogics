Enrollment Window

⏱ 60-day SEP window: Job loss is a Qualifying Life Event. You have 60 days from your last day of employer coverage to enroll in a Marketplace plan. Missing this window means waiting until Open Enrollment (Nov 1 – Jan 15) unless you have another qualifying event.

📋 COBRA election deadline: You have 60 days to elect COBRA after losing coverage, but your election is retroactive — you can wait to see if you need claims before electing, then pay back-premiums.
