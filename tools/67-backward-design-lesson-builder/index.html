Backward-Design Lesson Builder

Build lessons the way expert teachers think: start with what students must be able to do, then design evidence of learning, then plan activities. This tool scaffolds the Understanding by Design (UbD) framework — Stage 1 Desired Results, Stage 2 Assessment Evidence, Stage 3 Learning Plan — naming each step explicitly so you learn the method as you build.

📊 Framework: Wiggins & McTighe, Understanding by Design (2nd ed., 2005); Bloom's taxonomy updated 2001
🔒All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
Unit Basics Stage 1 of 3
Standards

Enter relevant standard codes and press Add. These anchor your desired results to curriculum requirements.

Essential Questions 1–3 recommended

Essential questions are open-ended, thought-provoking, and point toward the heart of the discipline. They should recur throughout the unit.

UbD recommends 1–3 essential questions per unit. More than 3 dilutes focus.
KLSU Grid — Desired Results

Fill each quadrant. The four quadrants are the backbone of Stage 1. K and L are foundational; S and U are the deeper targets.

K — Students will KNOW…
Facts, concepts, vocabulary, information students must recall
L — Students will DO… (Transfer Goals)
The skills, processes, or performances students can independently execute
S — Students will develop SKILLS…
Disciplinary skills and habits of mind built across the unit
U — Students will UNDERSTAND that…
Big ideas, enduring understandings — transferable beyond this unit
Performance Task Stage 2 of 3

A performance task is a culminating activity that asks students to apply their learning to a real or simulated situation. It should directly address the Essential Questions and reveal whether students have achieved the understandings in Stage 1.

Rubric Criteria Builder Up to 6 criteria

Add criteria for scoring the performance task. For each criterion, describe what performance looks like at 4 levels: Excellent, Proficient, Developing, Beginning.

Other Evidence

Check all additional evidence sources you will use to assess student understanding. These complement the performance task.

WHERETO Elements Stage 3 of 3

WHERETO is a UbD framework for designing the sequence of learning activities. Each letter represents a design principle. Fill in how each element will appear in your unit.

W — Where & Why
Help students know WHERE the unit is going and WHY. What is the big question? What will the final performance look like?
H — Hook & Hold
HOOK students at the start and HOLD interest throughout with engaging problems, mysteries, provocations, or experiences.
E1 — Equip & Explore
EQUIP students with needed knowledge and skills. Provide EXPERIENCES to build understanding directly.
R — Rethink & Revise
Build in opportunities for students to RETHINK big ideas, REVISE their work, and refine their understanding.
E2 — Evaluate
Build in regular opportunities for students to EVALUATE their own work and progress against the goals.
T — Tailor & Differentiate
TAILOR work to reflect individual student needs, interests, and learning styles.
O — Organize & Sequence
ORGANIZE and SEQUENCE the learning plan to maximize engagement and deep understanding.
