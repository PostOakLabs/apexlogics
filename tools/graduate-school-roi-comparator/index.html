Methodology note: NPV and IRR are computed over the full career horizon using differential cash flows (graduate school scenario vs. staying at current job). Opportunity cost = foregone salary during enrollment years. Post-degree salary is the user-specified target, applied from graduation year forward. Salary premium grows at the specified annual growth rate. All figures are nominal (not inflation-adjusted) unless discount rate approximates real cost of capital. Embedded data: NCES IPEDS 2023-24 tuition · BLS OEWS May 2023 wages · school-published MBA employment reports (Class of 2023).
