SECURE 2.0 Act — Unused 529 → Roth IRA Rollover (2024+)
If your 529 has excess funds after college, you may roll unused amounts into a Roth IRA for the beneficiary — up to $35,000 lifetime, subject to a 15-year holding rule (account must be open ≥15 years) and annual Roth contribution limits. Rollovers are tax-free and penalty-free. Source: SECURE 2.0 Act §126 (IRC §529(c)(3)(E)).
⚠ FAFSA Impact: 529 plan assets owned by a parent are counted at up to 5.64% in the SAI (Student Aid Index) calculation. Your projected balance at college start may reduce need-based aid eligibility. Use AL-17 (FAFSA SAI Simulator) to model the impact alongside your savings plan.
