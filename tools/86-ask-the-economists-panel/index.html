Ask the Economists Panel

Economics is not a unified science — it is a field where thinkers with fundamentally different premises about human nature, markets, and society have produced different but internally coherent frameworks. Put Adam Smith, David Ricardo, Thomas Malthus, Alfred Marshall, John Maynard Keynes, F.A. Hayek, and Milton Friedman in a room and ask them about the minimum wage: you'll get seven different answers, all logically consistent with their premises. This tool asks them.

Data vintage: Primary works: Smith (1776), Ricardo (1817), Malthus (1798/1820), Marshall (1890), Keynes (1936), Hayek (1944/1948), Friedman (1962/1980). Analytical frameworks compiled from primary and secondary sources.
