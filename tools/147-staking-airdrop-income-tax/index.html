Staking & Airdrop Income
Tax Impact Modeler

Staking rewards and airdrops are ordinary income at fair market value the moment you gain dominion and control over them, and that same value becomes your cost basis for the eventual sale. This modeler adds up your receipt events, walks them through the 2026 brackets on top of your other income, works out whether self-employment tax applies, and shows the deferred capital gain sitting in the basis you just created.

Data: 2026.07 vintage · Rev. Proc. 2025-32 · Rev. Rul. 2023-14 (staking) · Rev. Rul. 2019-24 (airdrops)
