Military Separation Details

Severance pay: involuntary separation = 2 months base pay × years of service (§1174). Voluntary (VSP/SSB) varies. PDRL retirement = 2.5% × disability rating × base pay. TSP withdrawals before 59½ incur 10% penalty + tax unless separation at or after age 55 (Rule of 55, IRC §72(t)(2)(A)(v)). Source: 10 U.S.C. §1174; IRC §72(t); 38 CFR (VA disability).
