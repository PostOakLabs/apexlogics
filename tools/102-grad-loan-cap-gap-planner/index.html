⚖ Legal & Policy Notice Federal loan caps per OBBBA 2025 (P.L. 119-21), effective July 1, 2026 for new borrowers. Grad PLUS is eliminated for new borrowers; graduate/professional borrowing is limited to Direct Unsubsidized within these caps, subject to an overall $257,500 federal lifetime aggregate (undergraduate + graduate combined). Existing loans are not affected. Private loan cost estimates use illustrative market rates — contact lenders for actual terms. This tool does not constitute financial or legal advice.
