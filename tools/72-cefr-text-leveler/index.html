CEFR Text Leveler

Before giving a text to language learners, know where it sits on the CEFR scale — and what would need to change to move it down a level. This tool analyzes a passage against A2, B1, and B2 readability indicators: sentence length, vocabulary frequency tier, grammatical structures, and text density. No AI — all analysis is rules-based and transparent, so you understand exactly what makes a text harder or easier.

📊 CEFR descriptors: Council of Europe (2020); vocabulary frequency bands: NGSL (Browne et al., 2013); grammar complexity indicators: Swan (2005)
🔒All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
