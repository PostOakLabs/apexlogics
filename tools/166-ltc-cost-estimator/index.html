Long-Term Care
Cost Estimator

Compares what care actually costs across four settings: paid help at home, adult day care, assisted living, and a nursing home. Set the hours of care needed, the local cost adjustment for where you live, and a cost inflation rate, then see the crossover point where in-home care stops being the cheaper option.
