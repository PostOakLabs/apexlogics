MBA Admissions Consulting
ROI Calculator

Admissions consultants charge $5K–$30K and claim 10–15 percentage-point improvements in top-tier admission odds. Is the fee worth it for your profile? This calculator converts the claimed probability shift into a dollar expected value using tier-level salary premiums and scholarship effects — then shows you the break-even uplift the consultant must actually deliver.

📊 2024–25 consultant fee ranges (comprehensive packages) · tier salary data from employment reports (M7 ≈ $175K base, T15 ≈ $155K, T25 ≈ $135K, T50 ≈ $115K) · uplift claims from published consultant marketing. All embedded.
🔒All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
