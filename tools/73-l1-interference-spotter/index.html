L1-Interference Spotter
(Mandarin → English)

Teaching English to Mandarin speakers means working with a specific, well-documented set of transfer errors — not random mistakes but systematic patterns that follow directly from how Mandarin works. This reference and self-assessment tool covers the major interference categories: articles, tense vs. aspect, topic-comment word order, classifiers, phonological transfer, and copula omission. For teachers and learners.

📊 L1 interference: Swan & Smith, Learner English 2nd ed. (2001); Li & Thompson, Mandarin Chinese (1981); contrastive analysis research
