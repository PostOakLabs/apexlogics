Real Estate · Ownership Cost

Total Cost of
Homeownership

The mortgage payment is usually less than two thirds of what a house actually costs you. This calculator adds property tax, insurance, maintenance, association dues, and the money you gave up by putting a down payment to work in a house instead of somewhere else, then nets out principal paid down and appreciation to show what the years you plan to stay really cost.

Data: 2026, user-supplied rates. Nothing is fetched; every rate here is one you enter.
