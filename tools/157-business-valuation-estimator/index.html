Business Valuation
Estimator

Three standard lenses on what a small business is worth: a seller discretionary earnings multiple, an EBITDA multiple, and an asset based floor. Set your own multiples and blend weights, and see how far apart the three methods land. The spread between them usually tells you more than any single number.

Data: 2026 (owner-earnings multiples are market conventions, user-overridable)
