Digital Assets · Tax Planning

Crypto Tax-Loss
Harvesting Calculator

Work out how much of your unrealized crypto loss you can actually use this year. Enter your lots, your gains so far, and your bracket, and the calculator applies the capital loss netting rules in order, caps the ordinary income offset, and shows what carries forward. Digital assets are treated as property, so the wash sale rule in section 1091 does not currently reach most tokens, but that posture can change and the tool lets you model it either way.
