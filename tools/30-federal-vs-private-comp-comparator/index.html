This comparator uses embedded approximations of federal pay scales and benefits. Individual results depend on your specific agency, leave category, TSP contribution level, health plan choice, FERS retirement tier (FERS, FERS-RAE, FERS-FRAE), and other factors. Pension NPV uses simplified assumptions. Consult your HR office, OPM guidance, and a fee-only financial planner before making employment decisions.
