Automation Payback

Every routine task you automate has a price tag on both sides: the hours it hands back times your loaded rate plus the errors it stops, against the build cost, the subscription, and the maintenance tax everyone forgets. List your candidates, and this tool ranks them by net monthly dollars with a break-even date for each, so you build the winners and skip the toys.

No tool-price database: hours, rates, build costs, subscriptions, and error costs are all your estimates, entered per candidate. The 20% maintenance haircut is the only fixed assumption and it is editable. data_vintage: user-supplied.
