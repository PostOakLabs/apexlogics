Parental Leave Income Gap Planner

Model the income gap during parental leave: stack employer paid leave, state SDI/PFL, and FMLA unpaid weeks. Find the monthly shortfall, savings required, and full return-to-work recovery timeline.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.

Your Compensation

FMLA (29 CFR §825): up to 12 weeks unpaid job-protected leave for eligible employees (12 months tenure, 50+ employees). State PFL/SDI varies: CA PFL = 60–70% of wages up to $1,620/wk (2025); NY PFL = 67% up to $1,177/wk; NJ TDI/FLI = 85% up to $1,055/wk. Federal employees: 12 weeks paid parental leave (FEPLA). FMLA and state PFL run concurrently.
