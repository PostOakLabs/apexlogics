You hold two or three admission offers. Which do you accept? This engine scores each offer on five weighted criteria — 10-year salary outcome by tier, scholarship money, cost of living, career-pivot access, and network strength — then runs a sensitivity pass showing which school wins under different priority weightings, so you can see exactly which value judgment your decision hinges on.

📊 tier salary trajectories from 2024 employment reports · metro cost-of-living indices 2025 · network/pivot scores by tier (heuristic, editable assumptions shown). All embedded.
🔒All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
