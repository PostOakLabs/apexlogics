About Policy Mandate

Policy Mandate is a structured JSON export format used across ApexLogics tools. Exporting your results as a Policy Mandate (JSON) file lets an AI agent carry your apprenticeship scenario — occupation, wages, ROI — directly into a downstream tool like the Credential ROI Suite (AL-02) or Workforce Board ROI Report (AL-07) without re-entering data.
