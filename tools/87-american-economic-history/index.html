AL-94 · Selected Studies — Economics

American Economic History Case Files

American economic history is a series of case studies in how institutions, technology, and policy interact to produce growth, crisis, and transformation. Five structured cases: the canal and railroad era, the National Banking Act and banking panics, the Progressive Era, the Great Depression, and the postwar boom.

Data vintage: Historical Statistics of the United States (Cambridge, 2006); Friedman & Schwartz (1963); Gordon (2016), The Rise and Fall of American Growth; primary sources compiled
