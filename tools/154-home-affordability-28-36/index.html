Home Affordability
(28/36)

Lenders size a mortgage against two ratios. The front-end ratio caps your housing payment at a share of gross monthly income, and the back-end ratio caps housing plus every other debt payment. This calculator runs both, takes the smaller answer, and works backwards from that payment to the loan and purchase price it supports. The interest rate is yours to enter, since nothing here connects to the network.

Data: 2026, ratios are lender convention, not statute; interest rate is user-supplied
