Scope-Creep Pricer

Scope creep leaks 10 to 25 percent of a fixed-fee project silently: the hours grow, the fee does not, and your effective hourly rate decays without anyone deciding it should. Enter the fee, the baseline scope you quoted, and the hours you actually worked, and this tool shows the rate decay, the unpriced leak, and the change-order price you should send at standard and rush multipliers, with a walk-away floor check.

Pure arithmetic on your own project numbers: the fee, the scope you quoted, and the hours you actually worked. No market feeds, no stored data. data_vintage: user-supplied.
