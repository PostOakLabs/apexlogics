30-Day Deadline — Irreversible

An 83(b) election must be filed with the IRS within 30 days of the grant/purchase date. Late filings are not accepted. Once missed, the election cannot be made retroactively.

What the 83(b) Election Does

Without election: You pay ordinary income tax on the spread (FMV − purchase price) as each tranche vests over — years.

With election (filed within 30 days of grant): You pay ordinary income tax on the spread today (often near-zero for founder stock at grant). From that point, any appreciation is treated as long-term capital gain — at a lower rate — once you hold >1 year from the grant date.

The LTCG clock starts at grant, not at each vesting date. This is the primary benefit for rapidly-appreciating startup stock.
⚠️ NSO note: For Non-Qualified Stock Options, the 83(b) election locks in ordinary income at the exercise spread now. The benefit is converting future appreciation to LTCG. Consult a tax advisor on your specific ISO/NSO mix.
