🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
How to use this tool
  1. Pick programs — Choose 2 to 4 MBA programs from the dropdowns below. Harvard and Stanford are pre-loaded as a starting point.
  2. Set your assumptions — Enter your current salary, target specialization (e.g. Finance, Tech), and preferred analysis horizon.
  3. Click "Compare Programs →" — The tool calculates total cost, opportunity cost, post-MBA salary lift, payback period, and 10-year NPV for each program.
  4. Review results — Programs are ranked by NPV. Use the side-by-side table and payback chart to compare. Export to Markdown, CSV, or AP2 JSON for advising handoff.
