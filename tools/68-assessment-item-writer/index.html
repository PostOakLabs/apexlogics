Assessment Item Writer + Distractor Analyzer

Write multiple-choice items that actually measure what you intend to measure. Grounded in Bloom's taxonomy and best practices from educational measurement, this tool generates MC stems and four options, then classifies each distractor by error type — so every wrong answer teaches something about what students don't yet know.

📊 Bloom's Revised Taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl, 2001); MC item-writing guidelines (Haladyna, Downing & Rodriguez, 2002)
🔒All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
