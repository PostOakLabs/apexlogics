Runway — months of savings at current expenses; 12+ months = max points.
Salary delta — pay increase adds up to 25 pts; a pay cut reduces the score.
Skill proximity — your self-rating of how transferable your skills are.
Market demand — your read of current hiring momentum for the target role.
