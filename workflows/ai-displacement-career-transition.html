AI Displacement & Career Transition

Face your automation risk honestly and build a transition plan. Screen your occupation, score your resilience, find the right credential, and evaluate whether a trade or degree path gets you there fastest.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
1
Step 1 · AL-111 · AI Displacement Risk Screener
Screen Your Occupation for AI Displacement Risk
Key InputsJob title or SOC code, years in current role, task composition (routine vs. judgment-heavy).

Start with an objective read of your automation exposure. The tool uses Frey-Osborne SOC-keyed probability estimates plus task routineness and AI-augmentation exposure to produce a composite risk score. It also returns the 3 adjacent occupations with lower displacement risk and similar or higher median wages — your pivot candidates for Step 4.

2
Step 2 · AL-06 · Career Resilience Engine
Score Your Career Resilience Against Growing/Declining Skills
Key InputsCurrent skills portfolio, LinkedIn growing skills benchmark, WEF 2025 declining skills list, industry, education level.

Displacement risk is occupation-level. Career resilience is personal-skills-level. The Career Resilience Engine scores your skills against LinkedIn's 2025 top growing skills and WEF's declining skills list to show whether your specific skillset is outpacing or trailing your occupation's risk. High resilience score means even a high-risk occupation may not threaten you specifically.

Open Career Resilience Engine →
3
Step 3 · AL-02 · Credential ROI Suite
Find the Credential That Maximizes Your Pivot ROI
Key InputsTarget pivot occupation (from Step 1), credential cost options, expected wage lift, time to complete, opportunity cost of study time.

Use the pivot occupations identified in Step 1 as your target roles. The Credential ROI Suite compares up to 3 credential paths (bootcamp, certification, degree) on NPV, payback period, and break-even wage premium. This is the step that tells you whether the retraining investment is worth it — and which credential format gets you there most efficiently.

Open Credential ROI Suite →
4
Step 4 · AL-36 · Trade vs. Degree ROI Comparator
Evaluate Trade vs. Degree Pivot Path
Key InputsTrade program cost, degree cost, time to complete each, starting wage for each path, 10-year earnings projection, debt load.

For pivots that cross the trade/degree threshold, this tool models the full financial comparison — debt at graduation, earnings trajectory, and cumulative NPV over 10 years. Many displaced mid-career workers find that a 12–18 month trade credential beats a 2-year associate degree on payback period, even when the degree carries higher long-term earnings potential. The tool shows you exactly where the lines cross.
