Step 1 · AL-02 · Credential ROI Suite
Calculate Per-Employee Credential ROI
Key InputsCredential cost (tuition, fees, time), pre/post salary lift, attrition rate change, time-to-productivity delta, reimbursement policy terms.

The Credential ROI Suite models employer investment in workforce credentials from the organization's perspective — cost per seat, productivity timeline, attrition payback period, and NPV over a 3/5-year horizon. Run this for the representative training program you're evaluating. Export the AP2 mandate to carry inputs forward.

Step 2 · AL-07 · Workforce Board ROI Report Generator
Benchmark Against Workforce Board Program Data
Key InputsProgram type, occupational target, state/region, cohort size, public grant eligibility, incumbent worker training vs. new hire.

State workforce boards fund employer training programs — often covering 50–75% of eligible costs. The Workforce Board ROI Generator produces a formatted ROI report that benchmarks your internal numbers against workforce board program cost-per-trainee standards and helps build the grant application narrative. Even if you're not applying for grants, the benchmark validates your internal assumptions.

Step 3 · AL-113 · Employer Training Investment Payback Calculator
Model Training Payback Across Three Return Levers
Key InputsCost per trainee, cohort size, productivity lift (%), attrition rate change (pp), promotion rate change (pp), average salary, margin rate, time horizon.

This is the board-ready output step. Enter your program cost and the three return levers — productivity, attrition reduction, and promotion pipeline improvement — to get payback month, 1/3/5-year ROI, and a cost-per-retained-employee metric. The tool uses SHRM benchmark ranges to flag whether your assumptions are conservative or aggressive relative to industry data.
