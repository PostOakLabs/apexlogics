Self-Employment Tax Optimizer

Three coordinated deductions that solo operators and S-corp owners routinely under-claim: the §199A pass-through deduction, S-corp salary structuring to minimize FICA, and home office + Augusta Rule business use. Run them in order — the QBI deduction is income-dependent, the comp split affects QBI, and the home office deduction closes the loop on Schedule C/K-1 income. All updated for 2026 OBBBA permanent thresholds.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or logged. Inputs disappear when you close the tab.
1
Step 1 · AL-144 · QBI §199A Optimizer
Calculate Your Pass-Through Deduction
Key InputsFiling status, QBI (net self-employment or S-corp K-1 pass-through income), W-2 wages paid, UBIA of qualified property, SSTB classification (consulting, financial services, legal, etc.).

Under 2026 OBBBA rules, §199A is now permanent with thresholds indexed to inflation: $201,775 (single) and $403,500 (MFJ) are the 2026 figures. Below threshold, you get 20% of QBI — no W-2 test needed. In the $75K/$150K phase-out band, the W-2 wage limitation phases in. Above the top of the band, SSTBs lose the deduction entirely; non-SSTBs must pass the W-2/UBIA test. The OBBBA also added a $400 floor deduction for QBI of at least $1,000. Run this first so you know what income threshold matters before optimizing S-corp salary in Step 2.

Step 2 · AL-145 · S-Corp Reasonable Comp Analyzer
Find the Optimal Salary/Distribution Split
Key InputsS-corp net profit, owner role/industry (for benchmark lookup), proposed salary, marginal federal + state rate, whether owner has Additional Medicare Wages above $200K/$250K.

The IRS requires that S-corp owner-employees pay themselves "reasonable compensation" — but reasonable has a wide band. This tool benchmarks your proposed salary against industry/role pairs (17 industry × 3 role combinations), calculates FICA savings vs. sole-prop SE tax, and scores audit risk (low/medium/high) using the Watson v. US (2012) ratio framework. The SS wage base for 2026 is $184,500. S-corp salary also feeds back into your QBI base from Step 1 — the tool shows the QBI trade-off for each salary lever. Additional Medicare surtax (0.9% above $200K single/$250K MFJ) is calculated separately from the employer/employee halves.

Step 3 · AL-146 · Home Office + Augusta Rule Optimizer
Claim Every Dollar of Business-Use Space
Key InputsHome office square footage + total home square footage, total home expenses (rent or mortgage interest + utilities + insurance + repairs), entity structure (S-corp or sole-prop), annual rent for your area.

Two deductions, usually modeled separately but better considered together. Home office: regular method (actual expenses × business-use percentage, limited to business gross income) vs. simplified ($5/sq ft, max 300 sq ft = $1,500 cap). Augusta Rule (§280A(g)): rent your home to your S-corp for up to 14 days/year — owner receives tax-free rental income; corporation takes a business deduction. The tool compares methods, identifies the winner, and produces a combined deduction summary for your tax preparer. Both methods include documentation checklists because the deduction is only defensible if you have the receipts and logs to back it up.
